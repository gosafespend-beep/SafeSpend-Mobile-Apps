Safe Spend is the private, all-in-one budgeting app that helps you track every dollar, pay down
debt, and actually reach your savings goals — without ever linking a bank account.

of where your money goes and a plan to keep more of it.

WHY PEOPLE LOVE SAFE SPEND
• Private by design — manual entry means we never touch your bank credentials.

BUDGET & TRACK

• Category budgets that show what's left at a glance

• Powerful search, quick edit, and undo

BILLS, GOALS & DEBT

SEE THE BIG PICTURE

IMPORT IN SECONDS
